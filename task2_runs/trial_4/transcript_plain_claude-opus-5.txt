Since the lobes are arranged around a central axis, I looked at the **angular position** θ = atan2(y, x) at times of large radius. For an N‑lobed attractor, the trajectory should "park" (spiral with large amplitude) at N preferred angles spaced 360°/N apart, drifting from one lobe to the next.
