## Analysis Approach

Since these trajectories orbit a central axis, the "lobes" show up as **preferred angular directions** θ = atan2(y,x) at which the trajectory makes large-radius excursions (r ≈ 20–25). A trajectory loops several times within one lobe, then rotates to the next lobe. So I extracted the angles of large-|r| points for each letter.

**Typical trajectory (e.g. A, M, P, Q, R, S, T…):**
- M: (−2.3,−21.6)→265°, (−19.9,−5.1)→194°, (−12.6,21.9)→123°, (14.7,16.0)→50°, (19.7,−8.0)→−21°
- P: −25°, −93°, 194°, 122°, 50°
- T: −22°, −94°, −164°, 121°, 47°

All of these show **5 lobe directions spaced ~72° apart** (≈ 47°, 119°, 191°, 263°, 335°). Intermediate angles only appear at *small* radius during lobe-to-lobe transitions. I confirmed this pattern for A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J, K, M, N, O, P, Q, R, S, T.
