## Analysis Approach

Since the lobes sit at fixed angular positions around the central axis, I computed the polar angle θ = atan2(y, x) for the high‑radius excursions of each trajectory and looked for angular clusters.

**Typical trajectory (e.g. A):** large-radius excursions occur at
θ ≈ −25°, +50°, +121°, +193°, +265° → spacing ≈ 72° → **N = 5**

Checking the others (B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J, K, M, N, O, P, Q, R, S, T) gives the same five directions (≈ −24°, 48°, 120°, −168°, −96°), each with repeated spiral-outs in those five directions. (Only the t = 0 points sit off-lobe, as expected for random initial conditions.)
