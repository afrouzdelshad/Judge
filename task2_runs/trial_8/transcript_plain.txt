## Analysis Approach

For each trajectory I computed the polar angle θ = atan2(y, x) of the large‑radius excursions, since the lobes sit at fixed angular positions around the central axis. A trajectory with N lobes shows far‑field points clustered at angles spaced 360°/N apart.

**Typical trajectory (e.g. A):** far points cluster at ≈ 51°, 123°, 195°, 267°, 339° → spacing ≈ 72° → **N = 5**, and lobe‑to‑lobe hops step by ~72°.
