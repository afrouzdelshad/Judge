## Approach

Since these are rotationally symmetric multi‑lobe attractors, the lobes sit at fixed angles 2πk/N in the (x,y) plane. For each trajectory I collected the polar angle at the large‑radius excursions and measured the **angular spacing** between successive lobe visits:

- Δθ ≈ 90° → N = 4
- Δθ ≈ 33° → N = 11
- Δθ ≈ 22.5° → N = 16
- Δθ ≈ 18° → N = 20
