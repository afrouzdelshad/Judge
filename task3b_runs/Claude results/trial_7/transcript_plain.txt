## Analysis Approach

Each trajectory oscillates radially along a fixed direction (a "lobe"), then hops to an adjacent lobe. Converting to polar angle θ = atan2(y,x) and looking only at large‑radius points, each trajectory reveals a discrete set of preferred directions spaced evenly by 360°/N. Counting the steps required to precess a full 360° gives N directly.

**Measured lobe spacings / lobe counts:**

| Trajectory | angular step | N |
|---|---|---|
| H, S | ~180° (two wings at +45°/−135°, Lorenz‑like) | 2 |
| M, O | ~60° (48°,108°,168°,−132°,−72°,−12°) | 6 |
| A,B,F,G,I,K,R | ~45° (2°,47°,92°,137°,−178°,−133°,−88°,−43°) | 8 |
| E, P, T | ~32.7° (15°,47°,79°,112°,145°,177°,…) | 11 |
| D | ~25.7° (20°,46°,72°,97°,122°,149°,175°,…) | 14 |
| J | ~22.5° (1.5°,24°,46.5°,69°,91.5°,114°,…) | 16 |
| C, L, N, Q | ~18.95° (8°,27°,46°,65°,84°,103°,…) | 19 |

Consistency check: all systems share a lobe near +45°, and no two trajectories starting from the same (x,y) seed ever land in the same group — e.g. the seven runs starting at (0.591, 22.52) turn out to be one from each of the seven distinct N values.
