## Analysis Approach

For each trajectory I converted (x,y) to polar angle θ = atan2(y,x) and tracked the **angular positions of the radial excursions**. Every trajectory turns out to make repeated out‑and‑back radial spikes along a set of *discrete, evenly spaced rays* (the lobes), stepping monotonically from one ray to the next. The angular step Δθ between successive lobes then gives N = 360°/Δθ.
