## Approach

For each trajectory I converted (x,y) to polar coordinates. The motion consists of a fast radial "excursion" (spiral out to r≈20 and back toward the axis) along a **fixed direction**, after which the direction jumps by a constant angular increment Δθ. The excursion directions therefore form a discrete set of equally spaced angles, and

**N = 360° / Δθ** (verified by counting the excursion directions over one complete revolution).
