## Analysis Approach

Each trajectory oscillates radially along a set of fixed angular directions ("lobes") evenly spaced by 360°/N. So I tracked the angle θ = atan2(y,x) at the large‑radius excursions and measured the **angular spacing between successive lobe directions**.

**Example (A):** sustained directions at 118°, 84°, 47°, 12°, −24°, −60°, −97°, −135°, −170°, 155° → spacing ≈ 36° → N = 10.
