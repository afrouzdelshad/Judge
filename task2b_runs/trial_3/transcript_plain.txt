## Analysis Approach

For each trajectory I converted the samples to polar angles θ = atan2(y,x) and located the **angular positions of the radial maxima** (the tips of each lobe excursion). In an N‑lobe attractor the lobes sit at fixed angles spaced 360°/N apart, so the consistent angular step Δθ between successive lobe visits gives N = 360/Δθ.

**Example (A):** lobe tips at ≈ 119°, 84°, 47°, 11°, −25°, −60°, −97°, −132°, −169°, 155° → Δθ ≈ 36° → N = 10.
