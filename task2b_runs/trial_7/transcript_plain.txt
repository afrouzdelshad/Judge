## Analysis Approach

For each trajectory I computed the polar angle θ = atan2(y, x) at the successive **radial maxima** (the outer tip of each spiral turn). Within one lobe the tip angle stays essentially constant; when the orbit hops to the next lobe the tip angle shifts by a fixed amount Δθ ≈ 360°/N. Measuring Δθ over several consecutive hops gives N directly.
