## Analysis Approach

For each trajectory I computed the polar angle θ = atan2(y,x) at each large‑radius excursion. In these systems the trajectory hops between **fixed angular sectors (lobes)**, so the set of excursion directions falls on a grid spaced 360°/N. Counting the distinct directions (or measuring the spacing and requiring the steps to sum to 360°) gives N.
