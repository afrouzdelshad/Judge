## Analysis Approach

For each trajectory I computed the polar angle θ = atan2(y, x) and tracked it over time. The dynamics show a clear pattern: the radius oscillates rapidly while θ **dwells at discrete plateau values**, then hops to the next one. These plateau angles form a uniform lattice with spacing 360°/N — this directly reveals the lobe count.

**Example (D):** plateau angles 10°, −25°, −61°, −96°, −133°, −170°, 153°, 119°, 83°, 46°, 12° → spacing ≈ 36° → N = 10.
