## Approach

For each trajectory I tracked the **angle θ = atan2(y, x) at successive radial maxima**. In every trajectory the point makes repeated radial excursions ("petals") at a discrete set of angles, occasionally hopping to the neighbouring petal. The angular gap Δθ between adjacent petals gives the lobe count directly: **N = 360°/Δθ**.
