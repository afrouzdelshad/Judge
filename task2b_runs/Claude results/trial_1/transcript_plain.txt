## Approach

For each trajectory I computed the polar angle θ = atan2(y, x) at the **radial maxima** (the tips of each lobe excursion). In every case the lobe tips fall on a set of discrete angular directions, and the trajectory advances one lobe at a time (always in the same rotational sense). The angular step between successive lobe tips is 360°/N, so N = 360°/Δθ.
