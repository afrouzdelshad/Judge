## Method

Since these are multi‑lobe attractors arranged around a central axis, each "lobe" corresponds to a preferred **angular direction** θ = atan2(y,x). Within a lobe the trajectory oscillates in radius while θ stays nearly constant; at a lobe switch θ jumps by ≈ 360°/N. So I extracted θ at high‑radius points, measured the angular step between successive plateaus, and counted how many steps are needed to complete a full 360° revolution.
