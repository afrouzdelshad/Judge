## Analysis Approach

For each trajectory I computed the polar angle θ = atan2(y, x) at the radial maxima (the outward excursions). In these systems the excursions land on lobes at equally spaced angles, and the trajectory steps to an adjacent lobe each excursion. Measuring the average angular step Δθ over many excursions gives N = 360°/Δθ.
