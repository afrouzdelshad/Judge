## Analysis Approach

For each trajectory I computed the azimuthal angle θ = atan2(y, x) at the moments of large radial excursion (lobe visits). In these systems the orbit dwells in one lobe (radius oscillates while θ stays nearly fixed), then hops to the neighboring lobe, advancing θ by exactly 360°/N. Measuring the angular step (and confirming it closes a full 360° cycle) gives N directly.

**Example (A):** dwell angles 119°, 83°, 47°, 11°, −25°, −61°, −97°, −133°, −169°, 155° → step −36° → 10 lobes (cycle closes after 10 hops).
